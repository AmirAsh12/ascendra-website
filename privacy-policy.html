Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Ascendra Academy ("Ascendra," "we," "us") operates ascendra-academy.co.uk and our associated LinkedIn, Instagram, and other social media pages. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

Who we are

Ascendra Academy is based in Leeds, United Kingdom. You can contact us at hello@ascendra-academy.co.uk with any questions about this policy or your data.

Information we collect

  • Website visitors: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited, and referral source, collected through standard analytics tools.
  • Social media followers and page visitors: when you interact with our LinkedIn Page, Instagram account, or other social profiles (following, commenting, messaging), the relevant platform shares limited interaction and performance data with us, such as post engagement and aggregate audience insights.
  • Newsletter or contact form submissions (if applicable): your name and email address, provided voluntarily.

How we use LinkedIn's API

Ascendra Academy uses LinkedIn's Community Management API through an authorized integration to publish content to our own company page (Ascendra-academy) and to view aggregate performance metrics such as impressions and engagement on our posts. This integration does not access or process the personal data of individual LinkedIn members beyond what LinkedIn provides in standard, aggregated page analytics. Publishing actions are only performed by authorized administrators of the Ascendra-academy page.

Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the basis of legitimate interest (operating and improving our content and services) and, where applicable, your consent (e.g., subscribing to updates).

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers who help us operate our website and publishing tools (e.g., hosting and automation providers), under agreements that require them to protect your data appropriately.

Data retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@ascendra-academy.co.uk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site usage. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
